What goes into a bar

Every batch starts on a stovetop, not a production line. Here's what we actually put in.

Glow Bar began as one person testing soap recipes at home, trying to find a formula gentle enough for daily use but strong enough to actually do something for the skin.

The current signature bar is built on a red wine base — chosen for its antioxidants — combined with niacinamide and kojic acid for brightening, and set using the melt-and-pour method so every bar cures clear and consistent.

  • Red wine base
    Rich in antioxidants; gives the bar its warm, wine-dark tone and a light, grape-skin scent.
  • Niacinamide
    Evens out tone over time and calms redness without stripping the skin.
  • Kojic acid
    Works alongside niacinamide to gently brighten dull or uneven patches.
